How to read the comparison

Recorded API cost: sum of Tibber consumption.cost. It excludes fees where applicable, production reward and Grid Rewards.

Hourly scenario: consumption in each hour × subscription priceInfoRange.total at that hour. These are subscription-specific prices, not a guaranteed quote for another tariff.

Monthly time-average: arithmetic mean of all hourly prices in each full local calendar month, multiplied by your consumption. This is a hypothetical comparator, not proof of your monthly contract rate.

The same variable-charge basis is used for the two scenarios. Fixed charges, rewards, tariff-switching conditions and invoice adjustments require separate reconciliation.

API history does not certify whether each observation is a measured reading or a profiled estimate. Confirm metering provenance before interpreting smoother consumption as a change in household behaviour.

Hourly resolution: this release requests hourly consumption and hourly aggregated prices. It cannot resolve quarter-hourly load shifting or exact settlement after a quarter-hourly tariff change.
